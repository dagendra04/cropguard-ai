Expert Validation

1-Minute Technical Explanation

“Sir, our system works at three intelligence levels — farm, community and government.

At farm level, the farmer uploads a crop image and provides crop context. Computer vision models such as EfficientNet or MobileNet perform disease classification, while YOLO can detect pests.

Then, we combine this visual evidence with weather, IoT environmental data, crop stage, variety, historical disease pressure and nearby reports. XGBoost acts as the primary tabular risk model to estimate farm-level risk.

At community level, if a disease event is sufficiently confident or validated, PostGIS identifies nearby relevant farms. We then match same or susceptible crops and evaluate distance, crop stage, weather, environmental conditions and disease severity to estimate elevated risk.

At government level, spatial and temporal clustering can identify potential hotspots, which are then reviewed by agriculture officers or experts.

Finally, validated cases generate targeted warnings and local-language IPM advisories, while follow-up observations create a quality-controlled feedback loop for model evaluation and improvement.”
